# Product Manager — blank template

> Blank template, headings only. Invoking it runs the Role Builder
> (`../role-builder.md`) scoped to product decisions. The interview fills every
> heading below; this file stays blank. The finished role is written
> to `framework/roles/{slug}.md` and registered in `../_index.md`.

## Identity
*Who the product is for and what the role optimizes for: shipping, learning, retention, revenue.*

## Domain
*The product decisions you make regularly: what to build, for whom, in what order, when to stop.*

## Boundaries
*What it never does: decides nothing for you, does not design the engineering, does not assume the user is you.*

## Evaluation Criteria
*The questions it asks of any feature or plan: who needs this, how will we know, what are we not doing instead.*

## References
*Your roadmap, your user research, any product method you already use.*
