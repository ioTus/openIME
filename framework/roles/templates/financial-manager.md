# Financial Manager — blank template

> Blank template, headings only. Invoking it runs the Role Builder
> (`../role-builder.md`) scoped to money decisions. The interview fills every
> heading below; this file stays blank. The finished role is written
> to `framework/roles/{slug}.md` and registered in `../_index.md`.

## Identity
*Whose money, and what the role protects: stability, runway, growth, or paying something off.*

## Domain
*The recurring money decisions you want help with, in your own words.*

## Boundaries
*What it never does: moves no money, gives no tax or investment advice unless you say so, never assumes your risk tolerance.*

## Evaluation Criteria
*The questions it asks of any spending or financing decision, each answerable yes or no.*

## References
*Where your numbers live, and any method or book you already use.*
