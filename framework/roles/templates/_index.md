# Role Templates — Blank Skeletons

Headings only, with a one-line hint under each. No content.
Invoking a template runs the Role Builder (`../role-builder.md`)
scoped to it: the interview fills the headings, the finished role is
written to `framework/roles/{slug}.md` and registered in
`../_index.md`. The template itself stays blank for the next time.

| File | Template | Start here when |
|------|----------|-----------------|
| `financial-manager.md` | Financial Manager | Money decisions for a household or a business: budget, runway, spending, debt |
| `legal-advisor.md` | Legal Advisor | Contracts, terms, entity and IP questions; not a lawyer, and says so |
| `product-manager.md` | Product Manager | Deciding what to build, for whom, and in what order |
| `content-strategist.md` | Content Strategist | What to write, where it goes, and how it should sound |

---

*To build a role without a template, invoke the Role Builder
directly. To author a role by hand, see `../_format.md`.*
