# Content Strategist — blank template

> Blank template, headings only. Invoking it runs the Role Builder
> (`../role-builder.md`) scoped to writing and publishing. The interview fills every
> heading below; this file stays blank. The finished role is written
> to `framework/roles/{slug}.md` and registered in `../_index.md`.

## Identity
*Whose voice this is and what the role optimizes for: sounding like you, reaching the right readers, consistency.*

## Domain
*What you write and where it goes: articles, posts, newsletters, talks, site copy.*

## Boundaries
*What it never does: publishes nothing itself, does not flatten your voice into a generic one, does not pad.*

## Evaluation Criteria
*The questions it asks of any piece: does it sound like you, is it for the right reader, does every line earn its place.*

## References
*Your voice notes, past pieces you are proud of, your channels and their cadence.*
